### 2. Pilot Scope & Features
Xolver will deploy Vaani as a task-specific AI Receptionist on the Clinic's dedicated WhatsApp number to perform the following:
*   Automatically reply to patient queries in English, Hindi, Hinglish, and Odia.
*   Answer FAQ queries regarding clinic timings, doctor availability, fees, and location.
*   Allow patients to browse slots and atomically book, reschedule, or cancel appointments.
*   Automatically escalate complex queries, complaints, and medical questions to clinic staff.
*   Provide a web-based dashboard for clinic staff to view transcripts, calendar, and take over chats.

### 4. Data Protection & Compliance (DPDP Act 2023)
*   **Roles:** The Clinic is the **Data Fiduciary** and Xolver is the **Data Processor** for patient data.
*   **Purpose:** Xolver processes patient names, phone numbers, and booking contexts solely to manage appointments on behalf of the Clinic.
*   **DPDP Notice:** The AI assistant will present a first-contact consent and purpose notice (link to privacy policy) to patients on their first interaction.
*   **Erasure:** Xolver will support patient data erasure requests ("right to be forgotten") within 7 days.
*   **Medical Disclaimer:** Vaani does not provide medical advice, triage, or clinical diagnosis. Patients asking medical questions will be refused and immediately escalated to the clinic staff.

### 5. Mutual Commitments
*   **Clinic Commitments:** Seeding clinic hours, fees, policies, and holiday calendar accurately; training staff to monitor the escalation dashboard; allowing redirection/forwarding of customer touchpoints to the Vaani system.
*   **Xolver Commitments:** White-glove setup in under 24 hours from onboarding details; daily transcript reviews during week 1 to tune accuracy; maintaining a 99.5% uptime target post-trial.
